## Description

Floater is a one-tap arcade game set under the arena lights.

Tap to rise. Let go to drop. Defenders reach down from above and up from the hardwood, and you have to slip through the gap between their hands. Every gap you clear is a bucket. Touch a hand or the floor and you're blocked.

• One-tap controls that are easy to learn and hard to master
• Haptic feedback on every jump and every bucket
• Your best score is saved on your phone
• No ads, no accounts, no tracking, works offline

Run it back as many times as it takes.
