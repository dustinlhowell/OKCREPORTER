# Floater privacy policy

_Last updated: October 16, 2026_

Floater does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

- The game has no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking.
- The only data it saves is your best score, kept on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.
- The game makes no network requests while you play.

Questions: contact the developer at the email listed on the App Store page.
